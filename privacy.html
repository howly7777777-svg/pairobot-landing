Privacy Policy

English

Effective Date: October 2026 | App Version: 3.0.0 (Build 28)

PaiRobot ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by Vajrapadma Development, is engineered from the ground up on a Local-First, Privacy-Sovereign Architecture. We believe your executive meetings, schedules, business receipts, and confidential communications should remain strictly in your control.

This Privacy Policy details how PaiRobot handles data across iOS (iPhone & iPad) and macOS, explicitly adhering to the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1), GDPR, and CCPA standards.

1. Local-First Architecture & Sovereign Data Storage

PaiRobot operates primarily on-device. All core intelligence functions—including audio recordings, extracted meeting minutes, action items, task schedules, and receipt records—are stored strictly within your personal device's local App Sandbox and Secure Enclave.

  • No Proprietary Storage Cloud: We do not operate remote multitenant databases that store, harvest, or aggregate your personal transcripts, schedule data, or tax records.
  • Hardware-Level Encryption: Sensitive credentials, personal signatures, and private notes are safeguarded using Apple's 256-bit AES Secure Enclave hardware encryption.

2. Device Hardware & System Permissions

PaiRobot requests specific system permissions solely to execute features you directly trigger:

  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Used strictly when you tap the record button to transcribe meetings or capture executive voice memos. Audio streams are processed locally or directed to your paired Mac Host; audio is never passively recorded or streamed without your explicit action.
  • Camera & Photo Library (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used strictly in the SoloFinance Vision OCR module to scan receipts, bills, and tax invoices. All text extraction is executed on-device using Apple's native Vision framework.
  • Calendars & Reminders (EventKit): Used strictly to inspect fixed calendar meetings for conflict avoidance on the SynapseOS timeline and to sync extracted action items to Apple Reminders with one tap. Data is read and written locally; no calendar entries are uploaded to external servers.
  • Local Network & Bonjour (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription): Used on macOS and iOS to discover and securely pair your iPhone with your Mac Desk Host over your private local Wi-Fi or LAN.

3. Mac Host Pairing & Local Offline AI (Ollama)

When you pair your iPhone with your Mac Desk Host, the communication occurs peer-to-peer over your local encrypted network:

  • Heavy AI summarization and contract review tasks are executed locally on your Mac's Apple Silicon neural engine running local Ollama (Llama 3, Mistral, or compatible models).
  • Zero Cloud Leaks: Under Local AI mode, 100% of the prompts, transcriptions, and analyses remain inside your private local network. Zero bytes leave your physical hardware.

4. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) & Optional Cloud LLMs

If you choose to enable cloud AI providers (such as Google Gemini or OpenAI):

  • You provide your own API credentials (BYOK). Your API keys are encrypted locally inside your device keychain and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • API requests are transmitted directly from your device to the respective provider's official endpoint via TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS.
  • We explicitly mandate that your data is never used for foundation model training by third-party processors.

5. Zero Sale of Personal Data & Zero Advertising SDKs

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information. PaiRobot contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user-tracking analytics libraries, and zero behavioral telemetry profilers.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All subscriptions and purchases are processed exclusively through Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit 2). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details.

7. Data Retention & User Controls

Because your data is stored locally, you maintain total ownership and control:

  • Export: You can export meeting minutes as Markdown/Text and tax receipt logs as standard CPA-ready PDF/CSV files at any time.
  • Deletion: Deleting an item in PaiRobot removes it immediately from local storage. Deleting the app clears all sandbox data from your device.

隱私權政策

繁體中文

生效日期：2026年10月 | 適用版本：v3.0.0 (Build 28)

PaiRobot（以下簡稱「本軟體」或「我們」）由 Vajrapadma 開發團隊打造。我們深知高階主管與專業工作者的會議記錄、行動日程、發票財務與商務機密具有高度敏感性，因此從架構底層即全面落實「本機優先、數據自主 (Local-First & Data Sovereign)」之最高隱私標準。

本隱私權政策詳細說明 PaiRobot 在 iOS (iPhone / iPad) 與 macOS 平台如何收集、使用與保護您的資料，並嚴格遵循 Apple App Store 審核指南（Guideline 5.1）、歐盟一般資料保護規則 (GDPR) 與台灣個人資料保護法規。

1. 本機優先架構與數據完全自主

PaiRobot 採取「端側本機優先」架構設計。您所有的核心資料（包括會議錄音、逐字稿、行動待辦、時間軸排程、發票影像與營業稅扣抵紀錄），均直接存放在您個人的 Apple 設備沙盒（App Sandbox）與硬體安全隔離區（Secure Enclave）中。

  • 無中心化雲端資料庫：我們絕不在雲端伺服器儲存、備份、剖析或收集您的個人會議紀錄與報稅明細。
  • 256 位元硬體級加密：重要商業備忘與機密憑據均受本機 Apple Secure Enclave 硬體晶片防護，需經 Face ID / Touch ID 或設備密碼授權解鎖。

2. 設備權限使用說明

本軟體僅在您主動觸發相關功能時，向系統申請必要的權限：

  • 麥克風權限 (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription)：僅在您點擊「會議錄音」或「語音輸入」時啟用，用於實時音訊捕捉與文字轉錄。音訊由本機或您配對的 Mac 主機處理，絕不進行背景無預警錄音。
  • 相機與相簿權限 (NSCameraUsageDescription)：僅在 SoloFinance 拍立得發票功能拍照或選取收據時使用。影像直接透過 Apple 原生端側 Vision 框架辨識文字，照片絕不上傳至任何外部第三方程式庫。
  • 行事曆與提醒事項權限 (EventKit)：僅用於 SynapseOS 動態排程讀取今日固定會議以智慧避開衝突，以及將會議決議行動項一鍵寫入系統「提醒事項」與「行事曆」。所有讀寫均在端側原生完成，絕不上傳雲端。
  • 本機區域網路與 Bonjour (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription)：用於外出 iPhone 與桌面 Mac 幕僚主機進行點對點安全搜尋與加密配對。

3. Mac 幕僚主機配對與本地離線大模型 (Ollama)

當您將 iPhone 與桌上型 Mac 配對時，兩端均在您的個人內部加密 Wi-Fi / LAN 環境下通訊：

  • 高運算量的商業合約審閱與深度會議拆解，直接在 Mac 的 Apple Silicon 神經引擎上運行本地 Ollama（如 Llama 3 / Mistral 等開源模型）。
  • 機密零外洩保證：在本地離線大模型模式下，百分之百的對話、逐字稿與分析結論皆保留在您的實體硬體內，零位元組上傳至公有雲。

4. 自帶金鑰 (BYOK) 架構與選用雲端大模型

若您自主選擇使用第三方商業大模型（例如 Google Gemini 或 OpenAI）：

  • 您使用個人的 API 金鑰（Bring Your Own Key），金鑰僅加密保存在設備本機，絕不上傳至本軟體開發者伺服器。
  • API 請求均由您的設備端直接透過 TLS 1.3 加密連線傳送至官方端點。
  • 我們明令禁止第三方處理器將您的機密資料用於其基礎模型之訓練。

5. 絕不轉售個人資料與零廣告追蹤

我們絕不向任何數據經紀商、廣告商或第三方機構轉售、出租、交換或共享您的任何個人資料。本軟體不包含任何第三方廣告追蹤 SDK、行為分析監控或跨 App 追蹤機制。

6. 應用程式內購買與訂閱處理

PaiRobot 的所有 Pro 方案訂閱與付費均完全透過 Apple 原生 In-App Purchase (StoreKit 2) 交易。開發團隊無法存取、亦不經手您的信用卡號、帳單地址或銀行帳號。

7. 數據保留、匯出與刪除權利

由於資料儲存於您個人的本機設備中，您擁有完整的處置權限：

  • 隨時匯出：您可以一鍵將會議逐字稿匯出為 Markdown/TXT，將發票明細匯出為符合會計標準的 PDF / CSV 報表。
  • 徹底刪除：在 App 內刪除任何會議或發票紀錄，本機即同步清除；若移除本應用程式，設備沙盒內的所有資料將被立即銷毀。
